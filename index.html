Coach Pick · On the field, where he's always been
Founder · Head Hitting Coach

Calvin
Pickering

Calvin "Pick" Pickering is a former Major League first baseman and designated hitter who spent 15 years in professional baseball — including five MLB campaigns with the Baltimore Orioles, Cincinnati Reds, Boston Red Sox, and Kansas City Royals.

Drafted by Baltimore in 1995 out of Tampa, Pickering tore through the minors as one of the organization's top power prospects, earning rookie-level Player of the Year honors and mashing his way to a big-league debut at just 21 years old.

His first two Major League home runs? Off David Cone and Pedro Martínez — two of the most dominant pitchers of the era. His pro journey later took him to Korea's KBO League and the Mexican League, giving him a hitting education few coaches anywhere can match.

"I find joy in teaching my son and other kids." — Calvin Pickering

That's what Blast Off Baseball is: everything Calvin learned standing in against the best pitching on the planet, handed down to the next generation of hitters.

Lesson Policy

We keep it simple: your player's session time is reserved just for them, and packages are built to create momentum. Here's how we protect both.

01
Cancellations & No-Shows

Give us 2 hours' notice

When you book a session, that time in the cage is reserved exclusively for your player. If you need to cancel or reschedule, just let Coach Pick know at least 2 hours before your scheduled start time and we'll take care of you.

Cancellations made within 2 hours of your session — or missed sessions without notice — will be charged as a completed lesson. For package holders, that session is deducted from your package.

02
Training Packages

Complete within 6 weeks

Consistency is where hitters are built — packages are designed to be used, not stockpiled. All training packages must be completed within six (6) weeks of your first session in that package.

Any sessions remaining after the 6-week window will expire and cannot be carried over or refunded. Book your sessions early and keep the reps coming.

Need to cancel, reschedule, or have a question about your package? Call or text Coach Pick at (949) 535-6369 or email blastoffbaseball@gmail.com.
